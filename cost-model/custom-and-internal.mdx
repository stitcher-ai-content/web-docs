Anything that costs money can be a cost dataset in the [cost model](/cost-model/overview). Internal IT — data-center, licensing, internal service charges — vendors without a dedicated connector, and one-off spend all enter as **custom cost datasets** and are normalized to [FOCUS](/data-foundation/focus) like a cloud bill. Business data arrives the same way and gives that spend its context.

## Three ways in

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Custom cost file" icon="file-csv">
    Parquet or CSV — already in FOCUS, or in your own column layout — read from file or blob storage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PDF invoice" icon="file-pdf">
    A vendor's invoice PDF — cloud, SaaS, or non-IT — mapped into FOCUS with AI-powered ingestion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business data" icon="sitemap">
    Ownership, cost centers, headcount, revenue, budgets — as files, warehouse tables, or versioned in your repository.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Custom cost files

You bring a custom cost file in through a **shared, multi-purpose source connection** to your storage account, then craft a cost source from it in the StitcherAI Editor. At the point of use you name the **provider** the spend belongs to and choose the **schema**:

| Schema | When to use it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **FOCUS** | Your file already uses FOCUS columns | Rows pass through as-is |
| **Custom** | Your file has its own column layout | You map your columns to FOCUS in the editor; the mapping is saved as part of your model |

Rows carry the provider name you assign, so the vendor's spend groups, filters, and allocates like any other provider's.

## PDF invoices

A custom cost source also accepts **PDF**. Through chat in the editor, StitcherAI reads the invoice, proposes how to map it into FOCUS, shows a preview, and lets you refine before saving it as a source. Known vendors have dedicated invoice parsers; a general parser handles everyone else — including non-IT vendors. The full flow is on [AI-powered ingestion](/data-foundation/ai-ingestion).

If you hold contract and invoice PDFs for many vendors, the **doc-organizer** utility files them into a vendor/year/month folder layout and gives you the sync command for your landing bucket. See [Contract Intelligence](/cost-model/contract-intelligence#the-doc-organizer-on-ramp).
</Tip>

## Business data

Business data is not cost itself — it is the context the rest of the model runs on.

| You bring | As | It is used to |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership, teams, cost centers | A file, a warehouse table (Snowflake, BigQuery), or a table versioned in your repository | [Enrich](/pipeline/model/enrich) cost with the right owner and attribute it |
| Headcount, seats, usage drivers | The same | Drive [allocation](/pipeline/model/allocate) of shared cost |
| Revenue, budgets, customer counts | The same | Give [KPIs](/pipeline/build/generate-artifacts) a denominator and [forecasts](/pipeline/build/events) a business driver |

Datasets, connectors, and the shared connection you craft sources from are described on [Datasets, connectors & data connections](/data-foundation/overview).

## How custom cost lands in FOCUS

<Note>
Guaranteed FOCUS columns — billing and charge periods, billed and effective cost, provider, service, currency, charge category — are populated for every custom source. Best-effort columns are populated to the extent the file or invoice carries them. StitcherAI does not manufacture definition that isn't present in the source. See [FOCUS](/data-foundation/focus).
</Note>

A signed contract is the fourth way in: when a vendor has no billing feed, StitcherAI can derive its cost from the contract itself. See [Contract Intelligence](/cost-model/contract-intelligence).

## Push your own data in

Because you own your data, you can push it in from your own side. Customer-side push tools write **any cost dataset to your own storage** — your own keys, your own infrastructure — where a StitcherAI **file source** then reads it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a push tool on your side">
    A customer-side tool (for example, one that syncs an AI platform's usage, or one that files your contract and invoice PDFs) runs with **your credentials, in your environment**, and writes files to **your own bucket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it with a file source">
    A StitcherAI file [data source](/data-foundation/connections-and-credentials) points at that storage and ingests the files like any other source — see [the data foundation](/data-foundation/overview) and the [supported integrations](/data-foundation/integrations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Your credentials never leave your side. The push tool runs in your environment; StitcherAI only reads the resulting files from storage you own.
</Info>
