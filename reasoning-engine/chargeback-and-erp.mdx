From your AI tool, the **FinOps Agent** turns the [allocated model](/pipeline/model/allocate) into a **chargeback** — what each team owes — packages each cost center's share as a **draft invoice**, and **submits the invoices to your ERP**. One natural-language request drives the sequence end to end, and you approve before anything is submitted.

<Note>
**Preview.** Chargeback → ERP is delivered through the [Reasoning Engine](/reasoning-engine/overview) in your AI tools (see [How to: Connect your AI tools](/getting-started/connect-via-mcp)), not through a web-app screen, and is currently in preview. ERP-side submission is available for **Zoho Books** today, with other ERPs following.
</Note>

## Attribution, allocation, chargeback

Three related terms, three different jobs. Chargeback consumes what the first two built.

| Term | What it does | Where it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Attribution** | Assigns each cost to the right party — team, product, cost center. | [Model → Enrich](/pipeline/model/enrich) |
| **Allocation** | Distributes shared cost to its consumers — double-entry and multi-level, so totals still reconcile. | [Model → Allocate](/pipeline/model/allocate) |
| **Chargeback** | Bills teams from the allocated model. | The FinOps Agent — this page |

<Info>
Chargeback reads the allocation status that Allocate records on every row of the model. If shared cost hasn't been allocated yet, the agent reports it as unowned rather than inventing a recipient — build the allocation first ([How to: Build an allocation](/authoring/recipes/build-an-allocation)).
</Info>

## The conversational flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for the chargeback">
    *"Run the August chargeback by cost center."* The agent confirms the environment and the period, then reports one row per cost center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drill in where a number looks off">
    *"Where does team-platform's number come from?"* The agent breaks one cost center down by provider — direct spend, cost allocated in, and credits allocated out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft the invoices">
    *"Draft invoices for that chargeback."* One draft invoice per cost center, with a line item per provider. Nothing leaves StitcherAI yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit to the ERP">
    *"Submit them to our ERP."* The agent finds the ERP configured for your environment and the matching ERP connection in your AI platform, submits, and returns the ERP's invoice references.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the report is built

The agent can group chargeback three ways:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="By cost center" icon="building">
    The primary report — one row per cost center, ready to invoice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By billing account" icon="credit-card">
    Each billing account treated as a cost center — useful before ownership tags are mature.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Provider lineage" icon="sitemap">
    One cost center, provider by provider: direct spend, allocated in, credits allocated out.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For every cost center the number decomposes the same way:

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Direct cost** | Spend the cost center incurred itself. |
| **+ Allocation in** | Its share of shared cost distributed to it by Allocate. |
| **− Allocation out** | Cost it originally carried that Allocate distributed to others. |
| **= Net chargeback** | The invoiceable amount. |

Two rules keep the report clean:

- **Materiality threshold.** Cost centers below the threshold roll into a single *below materiality* line rather than receiving their own invoice.
- **Unowned spend.** Cost with no owner lands in a default platform bucket for the FinOps team to resolve — it is never invoiced to a team by accident.

<Tip>
**Showback** is the same report without the invoicing step. Ask for it by name when you want to show teams their share without billing them.
</Tip>

## Draft invoices

Each draft invoice carries:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Invoice number | Stable for the cost center and period, so a re-run doesn't create duplicates. |
| Period | The month (or range) you asked for. |
| Bill to | The cost center and its owner. |
| Line items | **One per provider** — AWS, Azure, OpenAI, and so on — summing to the net chargeback. |
| Status | **Draft** until you tell the agent to submit. |

Below-materiality and unowned buckets are skipped; they are reported, not invoiced.

## Submitting to your ERP — Zoho Books worked example

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover the target">
    The agent reads the ERP configured for your environment and looks for the matching ERP connection in your AI platform — for Zoho Books, your Zoho connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or match a contact per cost center">
    Each cost center becomes (or is matched to) a Zoho **contact**, so invoices bill to the right party.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create one invoice per cost center">
    Each draft becomes a Zoho **invoice** against its contact, with the provider line items intact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    The agent returns the Zoho invoice references and totals so you can open them in Zoho Books.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Other ERPs.** QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, Xero, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance, and Workday are recognized as ERP targets; ERP-side submission for them is coming soon. Until then the agent produces the draft invoices and a submission plan you can carry out yourself.
</Note>
